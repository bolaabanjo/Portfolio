I turned 22 on December 17th. No grand celebration, no dramatic reset, lol just another quiet marker in a year that was anything but quiet. 21 was, without exaggeration, one of the best years of my life. I grew in ways I didn't think possible. I made real money. I didn't give up when everything in me wanted to. And now, standing at the edge of 22, I'm asking myself: what do I actually want from this next chapter?

## What 21 Taught Me

### I Can Do Hard Things

This year proved something I always suspected but never fully trusted: I'm capable of more than I give myself credit for. The projects I shipped, the challenges I pushed through, the moments I wanted to quit but didn't, they built something in me that no amount of theory ever could. Confidence is earned through repeated proof that you can handle what life throws at you. And 21 gave me that proof, over and over again.

### Self-Sabotage Is Real

Here's the truth: my biggest enemy this year wasn't external. It was me. The late nights that bled into early mornings. The inability to stop when I should have. The pattern of building momentum only to torch it with one reckless decision. Self-sabotage is insidious because it disguises itself as hustle, as dedication, as "one more thing." But I've learned to recognize it now — that restless voice that confuses destruction with progress. At 22, I'm learning to stop before I break what I've built.

### Not Knowing When to Stop

This one cuts deep. I don't have an off switch. When I'm locked in, I'm *locked in*, sometimes to my own detriment. I've pushed through exhaustion, ignored warning signs, and sacrificed rest on the altar of productivity. It worked, until it didn't. The crashes were brutal. The lesson? Sustainability isn't weakness. Knowing when to stop is a skill, not a surrender. I'm still learning it.

## What I Want Out of 22

### More Money, More Freedom

Let's be honest: money matters. Not for the status, but for the freedom. The freedom to build what I want, when I want. The freedom to say no. The freedom to invest in myself and the people I care about. 21 showed me I can make money. 22 is about making *more*, strategically, sustainably, and without apology.

### Even More Confidence

I'm already really, *really* confident. It's the byproduct of betting on myself repeatedly and winning more than I've lost. But I want to push that further. I want the kind of confidence that doesn't flinch in rooms I've never been in before. The kind that speaks first, not because I need to prove anything, but because I trust what I have to say.

### Embracing the Beginner

This is the hard one. I hate looking like I don't know what I'm doing. There's a deep, stubborn part of me that resists being new at anything. But 22 is the year I challenge that. Because avoiding beginner status means avoiding growth. It means staying in lanes I've already mastered instead of expanding into new territory. This year, I want to get comfortable being uncomfortable. I want to ask dumb questions, make rookie mistakes, and learn faster because I'm not protecting my ego.

---

21 was proof that I can build something meaningful. 22 is about building on that foundation — with more intention, more discipline, and a deeper understanding of my own patterns. I'm not chasing perfection. I'm chasing progress. And I'm betting on myself, again.

Here's to 22.
