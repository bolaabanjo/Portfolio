## What is Nicole?

Nicole is my personal intelligence network. Not an assistant. Not a chatbot. Not a tool. She's a system that learns who I am — how I think, what I know, what I don't — and grows with me over time.

Right now, she's an API wrapper with a scrappy local server. In 50 years, she'll run on her own hardware with her own model. Every phase builds on the last. Nothing gets thrown away.

## Why?

I want to be the smartest person in the room. Not through shortcuts — through depth. Nicole's first job is to make sure I actually understand what I think I understand. She questions me, exposes gaps, and forces me to think clearly.

But study is just the beginning.

## The long game

**Phase 1 — Study & research** (now)
Ingestion, Socratic dialogue, Feynman mode, research threads, writing critique. Runs on an idle laptop on my home WiFi. AI through Cencori. Zero cost.

**Phase 2 — Memory & identity**
She learns my patterns, my voice, my thinking style. Persistent memory that spans years. She stops feeling like software and starts feeling like a partner.

**Phase 3 — Communication**
Email, Twitter, WhatsApp, Slack, GitHub. She drafts in my voice, manages threads, surfaces what matters.

**Phase 4 — Senses**
Whisper for voice. Computer vision for sight. I talk to her. She sees what I see.

**Phase 5 — Physical world**
IoT, car systems, home automation. She manages my environment.

**Phase ∞ — Own intelligence**
Self-hosted LLM. Own training loop. Own data. Nicole stops being an API wrapper. Nicole becomes Nicole.

## Architecture

- **Offline-first** — works without internet, AI calls queued until connection returns
- **Model-agnostic** — all AI routed through Cencori, swap providers with a config change
- **Local hardware** — HP EliteBook "Banjo", 16GB RAM, on my home WiFi
- **Single database** — Postgres + pgvector
- **Designed to scale** — from one laptop to a private server, the architecture stays the same
