## The First Law

Energy is conserved. Always. The change in internal energy of a system equals heat added minus work done by the system.

$$\Delta U = Q - W$$

This is the foundation. Every reactor, every engine, every energy system obeys this.

## Internal Energy

Internal energy (U) is the total kinetic and potential energy of molecules within a system. We can't measure it directly — only changes in it.

## Why This Matters

When I was working on the QuanTonic Reactor, the first law set hard boundaries on what's possible. You can't get more energy out than you put in — but you can get *smarter* about how you convert it.
