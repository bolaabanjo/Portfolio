## Overview

The QuanTonic Reactor (QT Reactor) is a quantum thermal-to-electric energy system designed to challenge the limitations of conventional solar technology.

## Core Hypothesis

Conventional solar panels degrade over time and are limited by the Shockley-Queisser efficiency limit. The QT Reactor explores an alternative approach by leveraging quantum thermal dynamics to convert heat differentials into electrical energy.

## Current Status

Research phase. Exploring the intersection of thermal, quantum, and optical physics to realize intelligent and sustainable energy systems.
