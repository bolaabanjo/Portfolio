## Fluid Statics

The study of fluids at rest. Pressure at a point in a static fluid depends only on depth.

**Pascal's Law:** Pressure applied to a confined fluid is transmitted equally in all directions.

## Reynolds Number

The ratio of inertial forces to viscous forces. Determines whether flow is laminar or turbulent.

Re = (rho * v * L) / mu

- Re < 2300: Laminar flow
- Re > 4000: Turbulent flow
- Between: Transitional
