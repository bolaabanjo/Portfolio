## Problem

GPS-denied environments (indoors, urban canyons, dense forests) require drones to navigate using onboard sensors alone. Stereo cameras add weight and cost. Can a single camera be enough?

## Approach

Monocular depth estimation combined with a learned collision predictor. The system processes 640x480 frames at 30fps on a Jetson Nano.

Key components:
- Lightweight MobileNet-v3 backbone for depth estimation
- Temporal filtering to reduce depth noise
- A simple reactive controller that maps depth maps to velocity commands

## Challenges

- Scale ambiguity in monocular depth — solved via ground plane prior
- Latency budget is tight — every millisecond matters at 5m/s
- Sim-to-real transfer gap remains the hardest unsolved piece

## Status

Simulation results promising. Real-world testing planned.
