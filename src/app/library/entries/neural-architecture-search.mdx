## Motivation

Most state-of-the-art models are designed for data center GPUs. Deploying them on edge devices (phones, IoT, embedded systems) requires fundamentally different architecture decisions.

Neural Architecture Search (NAS) automates this design process, but most NAS methods are compute-prohibitive and hardware-agnostic.

## Approach

Exploring weight-sharing NAS methods with hardware-aware objectives:

- Latency as a first-class optimization target
- Memory footprint constraints during search
- Power consumption modeling via lookup tables

## Key Insight

The search space matters more than the search algorithm. A well-designed search space with the right building blocks will outperform a sophisticated algorithm searching over a poorly designed space.

## Status

Early exploration. Building benchmarks for comparison across device families.
