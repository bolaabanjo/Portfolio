This is not a book you finish — it's one you slowly absorb. Hofstadter weaves together mathematics, art, and music into a meditation on how meaning emerges from formal systems.

## What stuck with me

The idea of **strange loops** — systems that, by moving through levels, end up back where they started — keeps showing up everywhere I look. In recursion, in consciousness, in the way AI models reference their own outputs.

Gödel's incompleteness theorems alone are worth the read. The fact that any sufficiently powerful formal system contains truths it cannot prove is both humbling and fascinating.

## Why it matters

If you're working with AI or thinking about intelligence, this book is essential. It asks the fundamental question: can a system ever truly understand itself? I don't think we've answered that yet.
