Brian Greene has a gift for making the incomprehensible feel almost intuitive. This book walks through the history of physics from Newton to string theory, making the case that the universe's fundamental building blocks are vibrating strings of energy.

## What stuck with me

The explanation of **general relativity vs. quantum mechanics** — and why they break down when combined — is the clearest I've encountered. Greene makes you feel the tension that drives theoretical physics forward.

The idea that extra dimensions might be curled up at scales too small to observe is wild and beautiful in equal measure.

## Why it matters

I shelved this one not because it's bad, but because I want to come back to it with a stronger foundation in quantum mechanics. The ideas here connect directly to my work on energy systems — understanding the universe at its smallest scale matters when you're trying to harness it.
