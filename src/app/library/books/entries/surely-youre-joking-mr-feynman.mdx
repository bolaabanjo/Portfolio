This book changed how I think about learning. Feynman wasn't just a physicist — he was someone who refused to accept anything he didn't truly understand. That's a principle I try to carry into everything I build.

## What stuck with me

The stories about him teaching himself to pick locks, learning Portuguese to give lectures in Brazil, and questioning every assumption — these aren't just entertaining anecdotes. They're a manual for thinking clearly.

## Why it matters

If you're building anything — software, hardware, companies — you need Feynman's habit of asking "but do I actually understand this?" Most people skip that step. He never did.
